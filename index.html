The problem

EHRs are billing software.
They have no memory.

Every major practice management tool was built around billing codes, not clinical care. Therapists get the paperwork, not the intelligence.

📝 Blank page, every session

You remember what happened last session. Your EHR doesn't. Every note starts from zero — no context, no continuity, no memory of what was said 3 months ago.

⏰ Hours of unpaid work

Each session generates 16 minutes of documentation. 25 clients a week = 6+ hours of note-writing. All after your last session, when you're most tired.

🧩 Broken continuity

"What homework did I assign 3 sessions ago?" "When did sleep problems start?" Questions that take 20 minutes to answer from reading old notes now take 3 seconds.

How it works

The AI handles the busywork.
You handle the therapy.

1

Before the session

TherapOS surfaces a 90-second prep brief — recap, suggested questions, homework status, and goal progress. You walk in prepared.

2

After the session

Type your raw notes — a few bullet points, whatever comes to mind. Click "Generate Draft." AI produces a complete, clinically-worded SOAP or DAP note in 8 seconds.

3

Over time

Every finalized note deepens TherapOS's understanding of your patient. By session 10, it knows their arc better than any EHR ever could. Switching means losing that memory.
