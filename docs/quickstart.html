Getting Started

Quickstart

From signup to your first AI-generated SOAP note in under 10 minutes. No onboarding call required. No credit card needed for the trial.

⏱
Time estimate

Steps 1–3 take about 2 minutes. Steps 4–6 depend on your session length. Most therapists have their first AI note in under 10 minutes total.

Step 1 — Sign Up ~1 min

Go to app.therapos.com and click "Start free trial." Sign in with Google or your email address. No credit card required for the 14-day trial.

After login, you'll be asked three quick questions:

  • Your name
  • Your license type (LCSW, MFT, PhD, LPC, LMHC, etc.)
  • Your primary therapeutic modality (CBT, DBT, Psychodynamic, ACT, EMDR, etc.)

The modality setting affects your session prep brief questions and homework suggestions. You can change it globally in Settings, or set a different modality per patient if you use different approaches.

Privacy

Patient data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and never used to train AI models. Your 14-day trial includes HIPAA compliance — a signed BAA is available on the Solo plan. See Security Architecture.

Step 3 — Create a Session ~30 sec

Open the patient profile and click "+ New session". The session number auto-increments based on prior sessions.

  • Date: Today by default — change if documenting retroactively
  • Type: Individual (default), Couples, Family, or Group
  • Duration: Optional — useful for billing records

The session page opens. If this is your first session with this patient, the AI prep panel shows: "Session 1 — no prior history. Run the session and come back to generate your note." From session 2 onward, the prep brief auto-generates when you open the session page.

Step 4 — Run the Session

TherapOS stays completely out of your way during the session itself. It doesn't record audio or video. When you're ready to document, the raw notes panel accepts anything:

  • Bullet points during or after the session
  • Stream of consciousness shorthand — the AI handles structure
  • Full sentences or clinical shorthand (Sx:, Obj:, etc.)
  • Voice-to-text via your browser's built-in dictation (Fn+F5 on Mac, Win+H on Windows)

The AI reads everything you type. More detail = better note. Even 5–6 bullet points produce a good draft. You don't need to write in SOAP format — the AI formats it for you.

Step 5 — Generate Your First AI Note ~12 sec

After the session, with your raw notes in the panel, click "Generate AI Draft" (or press ⌘G). Choose your format:

SOAP

Subjective · Objective · Assessment · Plan. Standard outpatient format. The most common choice for individual therapy.

DAP

Data · Assessment · Plan. Streamlined format. Fewer sections — faster to review and edit.

BIRP

Behavior · Intervention · Response · Plan. Common for community mental health and group settings.

Free Text

No structure — AI generates a narrative note. Good for supervisors who prefer prose, or when SOAP/DAP feel constraining for the session type.

The draft streams token-by-token via SSE. You'll see it appear in real time. In 8–12 seconds, you have a complete, professionally-worded clinical note.

⚠
Always review AI drafts

The AI makes mistakes. It may misinterpret shorthand, use clinical language you wouldn't, or occasionally miss nuance. Read every field. Edit anything that isn't accurate. Your clinical judgment is always final — that's why it's called a draft.

The AI uses this information to generate the draft:

  • Your raw notes from this session
  • Patient profile — name, concerns, modality
  • Rolling AI summary from all prior sessions
  • Active treatment goals
  • Homework assigned and completion status

On session 1, only your raw notes and patient profile are available. By session 5, the AI has a rich treatment arc to work from. The quality improves with every session.

Step 6 — Finalize the Note ~1 click

When you're satisfied with the note, click "Finalize" (or press ⌘Enter). Finalization does three things:

  1. Locks the note — displayed as finalized in the patient timeline. You can still edit it via "Unlock to edit" but it creates an audit trail entry.
  2. Triggers embedding — your note becomes searchable via natural language search (⌘K) within 30 seconds.
  3. Refreshes the patient summary — the AI summary updates to include today's session. Every future prep brief and note draft will reflect this session.

The Aha Moment

Most therapists experience the product differently after 3 sessions with the same patient:

  • Session 1: The AI note is good but generic — it only knows the patient profile. You edit more fields than you keep.
  • Session 3: The AI prep brief is genuinely useful. It recaps last session accurately. It knows what homework was assigned. The note draft requires fewer edits.
  • Session 10: The AI understands the treatment arc. The prep brief surfaces patterns you hadn't consciously noticed. The note draft is 80–90% usable as-is. You've saved 2+ hours this week.

By session 20, the question becomes: could you practice as well without it? For most therapists, the answer is no — not because the AI makes decisions, but because the memory layer makes them faster and more thorough.

Troubleshooting

AI note generation is slow or fails

AI note generation depends on Anthropic's API. Rare outages happen. If generation fails, you'll see a friendly error and a "Try again" button. Your raw notes are always saved — you won't lose your input. Check status.anthropic.com if it fails repeatedly.

The prep brief is generic or says "no prior history"

The prep brief draws from finalized notes, not raw notes or in-progress drafts. Make sure you've finalized at least one prior session note for this patient. After finalization, the AI summary updates within ~30 seconds.

Natural language search returns no results

Search only works on finalized notes (they need to be embedded first). If you finalized a note less than 60 seconds ago, wait for the embedding job to complete. You can check embedding status in Settings → Data.

The AI included something I didn't write

This is called hallucination. The AI occasionally generates plausible-sounding content that isn't in your notes. This is why reviewing every draft is non-optional. If you see this, edit the field and submit feedback via the flag icon (⚑) next to the draft — this helps us improve.
