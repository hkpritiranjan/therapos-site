Getting Started

Quickstart

From signup to your first AI-generated SOAP note in under 10 minutes.

Step 1 — Sign up

Go to app.therapos.com and click "Start free trial." Sign in with Google or email. No credit card required. Your 14-day trial starts immediately with full access.

After login, you'll be asked for three things:

  • Your name
  • Your license type (LCSW, MFT, PhD, etc.)
  • Your primary therapeutic modality (CBT, DBT, Psychodynamic, etc.)

That's it. You're in.

Step 2 — Add your first patient

From the dashboard, click "+ New patient" or press ⌘N.

Fill in:

  • Name (first name is enough to start)
  • Primary concerns — e.g., "anxiety, workplace stress"
  • Therapeutic modality for this patient (defaults to your global setting)

DOB, diagnosis codes, email, phone — all optional. Add them when relevant.

Privacy note

Patient data is encrypted at rest and never used to train AI models. You sign a BAA with TherapOS on the Solo plan. See our security architecture for details.

Step 3 — Create a session

On the patient profile, click "+ New session". Enter:

  • Session date
  • Session number (auto-suggested based on previous sessions)
  • Session type (Individual, Couples, Family, Group)

The session page opens. If this is your first session with this patient, the AI prep panel will say "No prior history — this is session 1." From session 2 onward, the prep brief auto-generates.

Step 4 — Run the session

TherapOS stays out of your way during the session. When you're ready to take notes, the right panel has a simple text area. You can:

  • Type bullet points during or after the session
  • Write in any format — free text, shorthand, abbreviations
  • Use voice-to-text (browser native) if you prefer

TherapOS doesn't record sessions. Your raw notes are the input.

Step 5 — Generate your first AI note

After the session, with your raw notes in the text area, click "Generate AI Draft". Choose SOAP or DAP format.

In 8–12 seconds, TherapOS generates a complete, clinically-worded note using:

  • Your raw notes from this session
  • Patient profile and concerns
  • AI patient summary (from prior sessions, if any)
  • Active treatment goals

The draft appears in the note editor. Review every field. Edit anything that isn't right. The AI makes mistakes — your clinical judgment is always final.

Step 6 — Finalize the note

When you're satisfied with the note, click "Finalize." This:

  • Locks the note (still editable, but requires unlocking)
  • Triggers embedding — your note becomes searchable via natural language
  • Refreshes the patient's AI summary to include this session

The next time you open this patient before a session, the prep brief will include context from today's session.

The aha moment

For most therapists, the product becomes essential by session 3 with a patient. By then:

  • The AI prep brief is genuinely useful — not generic
  • The AI summary captures the clinical arc accurately
  • You've saved ~90 minutes of note-writing

By session 10, the question becomes: could you practice as well without it?
