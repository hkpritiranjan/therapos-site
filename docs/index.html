What is TherapOS?

TherapOS is an AI-native operating system for independent therapists and small clinics. It is not an EHR. It is not a therapy app. It is not another chatbot.

It is the AI copilot that therapists open before every session — and cannot practice without after 30 days of use. The product handles the clinical workflow that happens around therapy sessions: preparation, documentation, memory, and search.

Core Principles

  1. AI augments, never replaces. Every AI output is a draft. Therapist reviews, edits, and finalizes. The AI has no authority.
  2. Minimal clicks. The most common action should take the fewest possible steps.
  3. Everything searchable. Every note, goal, homework entry, and patient fact is findable via natural language.
  4. Every patient has memory. Patient context builds over sessions. The AI never starts from zero.
  5. Every AI action is editable. No AI output is ever final until the therapist says so.
  6. Therapist remains in control. AI surfaces and suggests. The therapist decides everything.

HIPAA Note

TherapOS is designed for HIPAA readiness from day one: encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), audit logs on all PHI access, role-based access controls, and signed BAAs with all data processors. See Security Architecture for details.
