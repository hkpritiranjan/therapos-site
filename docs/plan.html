§1 — Product Vision

TherapOS is the AI-native operating system for independent therapists. It is not an EHR. It is not a therapy app. It is the AI copilot that therapists open before every session — and cannot practice without after 30 days of use.

The product handles the clinical workflow that happens around therapy sessions: preparation, documentation, memory, and search. The AI remembers everything, prepares everything, and handles the busywork. The therapist remains completely in control.

The long-term vision: Every session makes the AI smarter about that patient. After 20 sessions, TherapOS understands the treatment arc better than any EHR ever could. Eventually, the question is not "can a therapist use TherapOS?" — it is "can a therapist practice as well without it?" The answer becomes no.

§2 — Positioning

Category: AI Practice Management — not EHR, not therapy app, not AI chatbot.

One-liner: "The AI copilot independent therapists open before every session — and can't practice without."

Unlike: SimplePractice (billing-first EHR), Upheal (transcription only), Mentalyc (AI notes without memory or prep), TherapyNotes (billing ledger with no AI).

Target ICP priority: (1) Independent US therapists — highest pain, highest WTP, fastest sales cycle. (2) Small US clinics (2–10 therapists). (3) Independent therapists in India — high volume, lower WTP, separate pricing.

§4 — Why Therapists Would Switch

They don't have to switch anything. TherapOS explicitly excludes billing, scheduling, and insurance. The pitch: "Keep SimplePractice for billing. Use TherapOS for everything clinical." Zero migration cost, zero switching risk.

The value is immediate: a therapist can generate their first AI note within 10 minutes of signup. The compound value (memory, patterns, search) builds over months and creates the switching cost that keeps them.

§5 — The Moat

Compound memory: Every finalized note deepens the AI's understanding of the patient. After 20 sessions, the context is richer than anything a generic AI can generate. Switching means losing that memory.

Workflow lock-in: Patient histories, notes, goals, and patterns are in TherapOS. The switching cost is not software — it's two years of clinical context.

Aggregate intelligence: As the platform scales, de-identified aggregate patterns across thousands of therapy arcs improve the AI for everyone. Individual therapists can't replicate this.

Speed advantage: Incumbents are billing companies. Rebuilding their data model to support clinical memory takes 2–3 years. TherapOS starts with a 24-month structural head start.

§26–29 — Design System

Visual identity: Dark, minimal, information-dense. Like Linear meets a clinical tool. AI is ambient — never intrusive, always editable. One accent color (indigo). No rainbow UI.

Palette: Background #0B0F19 · Surface #111827 · Accent #6366F1 · Text #E2E8F0 · Mid #94A3B8

Typography: Inter (system-ui) for all UI. JetBrains Mono for code and session numbers. Scale: 11/13/14/16/20/24px.

Stack: Next.js + Tailwind + shadcn/ui. Custom components: AiBadge, SessionPrepCard, NoteEditor (Tiptap), PatientTimeline, CommandPalette, GoalTracker.

§31–34 — Go-To-Market & First 100

Do the unscalable thing first. Find 10 therapists. Offer 6 months free for weekly 30-min calls. Watch them use it over Zoom. Fix every friction point within 24 hours. Ask for 2 referrals each. That's 30. Ask those 30 for 2. That's 90. Convert betas to paid.

Top channels: (1) Therapist Facebook groups — "Private Practice Therapists" has 80k+ members. (2) r/therapists + r/socialwork. (3) SEO content — "SOAP note template therapist", "how to write DAP notes faster". (4) Therapist podcasts — 20 shows with 5k–50k listeners. (5) Referral program — "give 1 month, get 1 month."

Launch plan: Product Hunt (Tuesday 12:01am PST) + Show HN ("I built an AI OS for therapists") simultaneously. Beta therapists share in their networks. Week 1: personal onboarding calls with every signup.

§35–36 — Metrics

Activation: First AI note generated <10 min from signup. Target: >70% of signups.

Retention: Week-4 retention target: >60%. Month-3 retention: >45%.

Business: MRR growth 15–20%/month early stage. CAC <$50 (organic-first). LTV >$500 (18mo at $29). NPS >55.

§37–38 — Risks & Regulatory

HIPAA: Encrypt at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Sign BAAs with all data processors. Audit logs on all PHI access, retained 6 years. MFA enforced.

FDA: TherapOS is documentation software. No clinical decisions, no diagnoses. Outside FDA SaMD Class II/III — as long as we don't add risk scoring that makes specific clinical recommendations. Consult a regulatory attorney before shipping any risk-scoring feature.

India DPDP Act: Store Indian user data in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1). Explicit consent for health data. Data localization compliance.

Biggest risk: AI generates inaccurate clinical content. Mitigation: always a draft, mandatory edit step, never auto-finalize, clear AI disclosure. The system is designed so the AI has no authority — the therapist decides everything.

§39 — How AI Should Behave

  • Always a draft. Every AI output is explicitly labeled. Finalizing requires a deliberate click.
  • Never fabricate. If information is missing, write "[Therapist to complete]" — never invent clinical details.
  • No diagnosis. No DSM codes, no clinical diagnoses in AI outputs. Prompts explicitly prohibit this.
  • Editable at every step. Every AI-generated field is a plain text editor. Therapist can delete the entire draft.
  • Transparent. All AI content shows a ✦ AI indicator. Audit log records AI generation events separately from therapist edits.
  • Risk nudge only. If note content suggests imminent risk, the AI shows a soft nudge: "Content may warrant a safety review." Never a diagnosis, never an alarm.
