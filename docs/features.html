Features

Every feature in TherapOS exists to answer one question: how can AI remove work instead of creating it?

AI Session Notes (SOAP & DAP)

The core feature. Type your raw session notes — bullet points, shorthand, stream of consciousness. Click "Generate Draft." In 8–12 seconds, TherapOS produces a complete, professionally-worded clinical note.

  • Formats supported: SOAP (Subjective / Objective / Assessment / Plan), DAP (Data / Assessment / Plan), BIRP, and Free Text
  • Context-aware: The AI reads your patient's history, goals, and recent sessions before drafting. The note reflects continuity, not just today's session.
  • Always a draft: Every field is editable. The AI never finalizes anything. You click "Finalize" when you're satisfied.
  • AI indicator: A subtle AI Draft badge marks AI-generated content so you always know what was generated vs. what you wrote.

Session Prep Brief

Before every session, TherapOS generates a 90-second preparation brief that surfaces what actually matters from the patient's history.

  • Recap: 2–3 sentences on what happened last session and the key themes that emerged
  • Suggested questions: 3–5 questions tailored to your therapeutic modality and current treatment arc
  • Homework check-in: How to approach checking in on assigned homework (and whether it was completed)
  • Cross-session patterns: Notable patterns the AI has detected across recent sessions (e.g., "anxiety spikes in sessions following workplace events")

Auto-generated before every session. Available on the session page. Takes less than 2 minutes to read.

Patient Memory (Rolling AI Summary)

After every finalized note, TherapOS regenerates a 3–5 sentence AI summary of the patient's clinical arc. This summary is injected into every subsequent AI call — making every prep brief and note draft smarter over time.

The summary captures: what's improved, what persists, recent breakthroughs, patterns, and current clinical picture. It never replaces the session notes — it complements them.

Natural Language Search

Press ⌘K and ask anything:

  • "What coping strategies have worked for Maya?"
  • "When did sleep problems start for James?"
  • "What homework has David completed vs. skipped?"
  • "Show all sessions where we discussed his mother."

Search uses vector similarity (pgvector) across all finalized session notes. Results include the relevant snippet, session number, and date. Filter by patient or search across your entire practice.

Treatment Goals

Set treatment goals per patient. Track progress across sessions. See the entire goal arc at a glance on the patient profile.

  • Create goals with a title, category (anxiety, relationships, sleep, etc.), and description
  • Add progress notes to any goal — linked to a specific session
  • Mark goals as Achieved, Paused, or Abandoned
  • Goals appear in the AI session prep brief so the AI knows what you're working toward

Homework Generator

After a session, click "Suggest Homework." TherapOS generates 3 options based on the session's themes, active goals, and your therapeutic modality. Each includes:

  • A title and detailed patient-facing description
  • Category (journaling, behavioral, exposure, mindfulness, social)
  • Rationale explaining why this fits the session and modality

Choose one, edit it, or write your own. Track completion in the next session.

Patient Timeline

Every patient has a chronological timeline showing all sessions, goals created, and homework assigned. Each session entry shows: session number, date, status (scheduled / completed / cancelled), and a note snippet if finalized. Click any session to open the full note.

Dashboard

The first thing you see when you open TherapOS. Shows:

  • Today's sessions with scheduled times
  • AI snapshot for the next upcoming session
  • Recently viewed patients for quick navigation
  • Quick actions: new patient, new session

Command Palette (⌘K)

Global search and action launcher. Search by patient name, session date, or note content. Launch actions: "New patient," "New session." Accessible from anywhere in the app.
