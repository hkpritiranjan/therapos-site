Source Register

One maintained record for every market, competitor and regulatory claim the docs rely on. Pages cite records by ID, so a price or legal fact is updated in one place: data/sources.json.

ℹ
How this stays current

Each record carries a cadence: competitor prices and features every 30 days, legal and regulatory items every 90 days, market statistics every 180 days. A "Review due" flag appears in your browser when a next-check date has passed. Nothing monitors sources automatically. Updating means: open the URL, confirm or correct the claim, change verified in the JSON, rerun python3 tools/enhance.py && python3 tools/build.py && python3 tools/enhance.py && python3 tools/check.py.

Known research gaps

  • No published time-per-note figure for therapists (E-TIME).
  • Private-practice share by profession; BLS excludes many self-employed.
  • TherapyNotes and several SimplePractice pages returned 403 to our fetch; those claims are Reported.
  • Upheal, Blueprint and Mentalyc client-facing features not tested hands-on.
  • India counts conflict; MyKaya, Cognixa, PsychDesk and PractiPal were not verifiable.
  • All statute texts for state AI laws were read through summaries, not the statutes.
