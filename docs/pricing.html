The ROI Calculation

A solo therapist with 25 clients/week spends approximately 10 hours/week on documentation at 16 minutes/session. TherapOS reduces this to ~3 hours — saving 7 hours/week.

The math

At $150/session, 7 recovered hours = ~$1,050 of billable time per week that was previously spent writing notes. TherapOS costs $29/month. ROI is not the question.

FAQs

Do I need to leave SimplePractice?

No. TherapOS deliberately excludes billing, insurance, and scheduling — the things SimplePractice handles. Use SimplePractice for billing, use TherapOS for everything clinical. They coexist.

Is the BAA included?

Yes. All Solo and Clinic subscribers receive a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) as required for HIPAA compliance. The BAA is available in Settings → Privacy.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export all your patient data as JSON or PDF at any time in Settings → Export. After cancellation, your data is retained for 30 days, then anonymized (audit logs are retained for 6 years per HIPAA requirements, but all PHI is removed).

Is there a free plan?

TherapOS offers a 14-day free trial with full access. There is no ongoing free plan — we need to keep the lights on and the AI running.
